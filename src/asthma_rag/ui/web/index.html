    About this tool

    Aspra is an educational prototype for exploring retrieval-augmented generation over public asthma guideline documents. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

    Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis or treatment decisions. In a breathing emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

    Every answer is retrieved from indexed guideline text, reranked for relevance, and passed through a safety judge before it reaches you. The confidence label in the session panel reflects the judge's assessment, not a clinical grade.
